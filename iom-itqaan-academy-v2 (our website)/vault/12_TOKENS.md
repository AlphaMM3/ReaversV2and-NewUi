**Priority:** Low until screen flows/roles validated. Brand colors, typography and UI system are not yet approved.

## Design principles
- Mobile-first, readable Bangla + English typography, accessible contrast, clear focus/keyboard states.
- Few primary actions per screen, progressive disclosure, plain error messages, no decorative complexity.
- Reusable components: AppShell, PageHeader, DataTable, FilterBar, ApprovalPanel, TaskCard, Modal, FormField, StatusText, AuditHistory.
